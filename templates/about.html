Project Overview & Objectives

About The Local Job & Skill Matching Portal

A dedicated technology platform designed to bridge the gap between daily wage workers and local employers through transparent, skill-based, and location-aware matching.

The Daily Wage Worker Dilemma

Millions of informal and daily wage workers face daily uncertainty and severe structural challenges in the unorganized labor market:

  • Gathering at Labor Chowks: Workers must assemble at street corners early each morning regardless of rain or scorching heat, often returning home without work.
  • Middlemen & Wage Exploitation: Unauthorized labor contractors and agents take heavy commission cuts from workers' daily wages.
  • Commute & Distance Burdens: Workers often travel long distances blindly without knowing whether suitable work is actually available.
  • Lack of Verified Track Record: No digital portfolio to showcase skills in painting, carpentry, masonry, or electrical work.

Challenges Faced by Employers

Local businesses, agricultural estate managers, and homeowners experience significant friction when hiring workers:

  • Shortage of On-Demand Labor: Difficulty finding skilled technicians (plumbers, electricians, masons) for immediate or short-term work.
  • Unreliable Referrals: Relying on word-of-mouth leads to unpredictable skills, tardiness, and delays in project timelines.
  • Inflated Agency Margins: Commercial contractors markup wage costs heavily without guaranteeing quality.
  • Inefficient Coordination: No unified dashboard to track job postings, review applicants, and confirm availability.
The Architectural Formula

SKILL + LOCATION = BETTER JOB MATCHING

Our platform replaces random searching with intelligent, rule-based algorithmic matching. By evaluating the trade skills of the worker against job requirements and calculating geographic proximity, we deliver instant, high-compatibility matches for both parties.

Key Project Objectives

Built as a real-world software engineering solution for local employment dignity

Dignified Digital Identity

Provide every daily wage worker with a verified digital profile showcasing their core vocational trade skills, years of experience, and expected daily wage.

Hyper-Local Connectivity

Prioritize job postings situated in the same taluk, town, or neighborhood (e.g. Bantwal, Mangalore), minimizing travel times and maximizing worker earnings.

Streamlined Hiring Pipeline

Equip employers with an intuitive dashboard to post jobs, monitor applications in real-time, accept or decline candidates, and directly initiate telephone contact.

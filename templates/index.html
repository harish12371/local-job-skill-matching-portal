How The Portal Works

A streamlined, 3-step process connecting workers and employers directly

1. Register Profile

Workers specify their vocational skills, wage expectations, and location. Employers set up company details and work locations.

2. Smart Matching

Our rule-based engine scores jobs based on matching skills, location proximity, and wage alignment so you only see relevant work.

3. Direct Connection

Apply with one click. Employers review applications and contact suitable workers directly by phone with no intermediary.

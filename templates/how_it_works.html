Step-by-Step Guide

How The Portal Works

A simple, secure, and transparent digital workflow connecting daily wage laborers and work providers directly.

For Workers / Employees

Find nearby daily wage work matching your specific trade
Register as Worker
Step 1
Create Profile

Register with your name, phone number, town/location (e.g., Bantwal), list your trade skills (e.g. Painting, Masonry), and set your expected daily wage.

Step 2
Discover Matching Jobs

Visit your dashboard to view personalized recommended jobs. Our engine ranks opportunities where your skills and location align best.

Step 3
Apply with One Click

Click "Apply Now" and submit a brief note. The system protects against duplicate submissions and alerts the employer immediately.

Step 4
Direct Call & Start Work

Once accepted, the employer calls your mobile directly to confirm the job location, timing, and wage payment. No commissions!

For Employers / Work Providers

Hire reliable, skilled local workers in minutes
Register as Employer
Step 1
Register Company

Sign up with your business or individual name, phone number, and primary site location (e.g., Mangalore or Bantwal).

Step 2
Publish Job Opening

Fill out the job form with title, category, daily wage offered, work duration, required skills, and number of workers needed.

Step 3
Review Applicant Profiles

View real applicant profiles on your dashboard. Inspect their skills, experience years, and wage expectations side by side.

Step 4
Accept & Connect Directly

Mark applications as "Accepted" or "Rejected" with immediate status feedback. Call the worker directly to coordinate work.
